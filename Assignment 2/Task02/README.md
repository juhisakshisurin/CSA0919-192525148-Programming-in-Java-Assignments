## Task objective

Compare `ArrayList` and `LinkedList` for the Music Playlist Editor operations in Task 2:

- Adding at the end
- Inserting and removing at the front
- Inserting and removing in the middle
- Accessing the k-th song
- Iterating through the complete playlist

The benchmark uses playlist sizes of 500, 5,000 and 20,000 songs. It also compares `ArrayList`, `LinkedList` and `ArrayDeque` for the changed Up Next queue condition.

## Result summary

- `ArrayList` is a reasonable choice for the main playlist when indexed access and iteration are frequent and front/middle edits are occasional.
- `LinkedList` supports efficient changes at the ends, but index-based middle access and edits require traversal.
- `ArrayDeque` is recommended for the changed Up Next workload because it supports efficient operations at both ends.
